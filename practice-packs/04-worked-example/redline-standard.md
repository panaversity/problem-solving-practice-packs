# Customer-side redline standard for vendor MSAs

> Our firm's baseline expectations when reviewing inbound vendor MSAs. Any deviation **away from** these positions must be flagged; deviations more favorable to Customer than this standard are noted but not contested.

## 1. Term and renewal

- Initial Term: **one (1) year**, not three. Three-year Initial Terms are accepted only with a documented business case and an at-cost early-termination right.
- Auto-renewal: opt-in only. Reject any provision that **automatically renews** without affirmative Customer consent. If accepted, non-renewal notice window must be **thirty (30) days**, not ninety.

## 2. Fees and payment

- Payment terms: **Net 30**, not Net 15.
- Late-payment interest: capped at one percent (1%) per month.
- Annual fee escalators: capped at **the lesser of CPI or five percent (5%)**, not fifteen percent.

## 3. Intellectual property

- Customer Data is and remains the property of Customer. Vendor receives a limited license to use Customer Data **solely to provide the Services to Customer**.
- Reject any clause that grants Vendor rights to feedback or suggestions in perpetuity. Feedback may be used by Vendor but title remains with Customer.
- Aggregated and de-identified use of Customer Data: **must require Customer's prior written consent**, not be granted by default.

## 4. Confidentiality

- Confidentiality term: **five (5) years post-termination**, not two. Trade secrets indefinitely.

## 5. Warranties

- Vendor warrants Services will materially conform to the documentation **and the applicable SOW**, not merely be "performed in a workmanlike manner".
- Reject blanket disclaimers of implied warranties. Implied warranty of fitness for purpose must survive at least for the Hypercare / acceptance window.

## 6. Limitation of liability

- Cap floor: **the greater of (i) twelve (12) months of fees paid OR (ii) $1,000,000** — not three months.
- Carve-outs **must** include: (a) breach of confidentiality, (b) breach of data protection or privacy obligations, (c) IP indemnification, (d) gross negligence or willful misconduct.
- Consequential-damages waiver is acceptable only if symmetric and subject to the carve-outs above.

## 7. Indemnification

- IP indemnification must include the right to **recover damages**, not merely Vendor's "modify / procure / refund" options.
- IP indemnification scope must include **U.S. and EU** patents/copyrights, not U.S. only.

## 8. Termination

- Customer must retain the right to **terminate for convenience** with sixty (60) days notice after the first ninety (90) days, against a pro-rated refund of pre-paid unused fees.
- Cure period for material breach: thirty (30) days is acceptable; non-payment cure period is fifteen (15) business days.

## 9. Data return and deletion

- Customer Data export must be available for **at least ninety (90) days** post-termination, in a documented machine-readable format (CSV or JSON at minimum), at **no charge**.
- Vendor must certify deletion in writing within thirty (30) days after the export window closes.

## 10. Governing law and disputes

- Acceptable forums: Delaware, New York, or the Customer's home state.
- Mandatory arbitration is acceptable only if (a) it allows injunctive relief in court, and (b) it does not include a "loser pays" attorneys' fees provision.

## 11. Assignment

- **Symmetric.** Either party may assign in connection with a merger or acquisition with notice. Asymmetric assignment clauses are rejected.
