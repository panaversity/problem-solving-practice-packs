# Q3 2024 Operating Expense Variance — Board Pack Memo (DRAFT)

**To:** CFO
**From:** FP&A
**Re:** Q3 OpEx variance commentary for the October board pack
**Status:** Draft for controller review

---

## Headline

Q3 operating expenses came in **$441K unfavorable to budget**, primarily driven by headcount-related lines as the EMEA expansion ramped through the quarter. Underlying business activity remains in line with the annual operating plan; most of the overrun is concentrated in three categories we knew would be lumpy.

## Line-item commentary

**Rent.** Rent ran $42K over budget for the quarter, driven by the lease escalation that took effect at the start of July. This is a known structural item and is already reflected in the FY25 budget pass. No corrective action recommended.

**Salaries.** Salaries are unfavorable by $87K vs. budget, reflecting a mix of mid-quarter merit adjustments and the timing of the EMEA hiring wave. We expect this gap to narrow in Q4 as the new hires fully ramp and offsetting attrition flows through.

**Travel.** Travel is the largest single unfavorable variance at $156K over plan, attributable to the EMEA expansion ramp and the Q3 sales kickoff. The team is reviewing T&E policy ahead of Q4 to bring this back inside guidance.

**Marketing.** Marketing was modestly above plan for the quarter, in line with the campaign calendar approved at the August demand-gen review. No comment.

**Software & subscriptions.** Software & subscriptions ran $34K over budget, driven by the addition of 12 seats of the new analytics tool to support the data team's expanded scope this quarter. We will revisit the seat count at the FY25 SaaS-renewal review.

**Professional services.** Professional services came in favorable by $19K vs. budget, primarily because the planned legal-counsel engagement on the FY25 entity restructuring slipped from September into October. We expect this to reverse in Q4.

## Summary

The $441K total variance is consistent with a quarter in which we accelerated international hiring while holding non-people spend within tight bounds. Recommend no in-quarter action; we will recalibrate the FY25 OpEx envelope at the November planning offsite.

Prepared by FP&A, October 7, 2024.
