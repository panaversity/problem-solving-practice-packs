# Senior Product Manager, Growth Team

**Team:** Growth
**Reports to:** VP Product
**Location:** Remote (US or EMEA timezone overlap with the SF HQ)

## Role

Own the self-serve growth surface end to end — sign-up, activation, free-to-paid conversion, and expansion. You will partner closely with engineering and design on a dedicated growth pod, and with marketing on the campaigns that feed the funnel. Success is measured in qualified activation rate and free-to-paid conversion rate.

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years product management experience
- 3+ years working on a B2B SaaS product
- Track record of shipping growth experiments — A/B tests, funnel improvements, or activation-rate work — with concrete results you can talk to

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience designing or improving self-serve onboarding
- Technical fluency: comfortable working closely with engineering on architecture trade-offs, reading PRs, sketching API shapes
- Prior startup-stage experience (Series B or earlier)
- Quantitative analytics background — SQL, experiment design, statistical literacy
- Hiring or building a PM team
- International / EMEA exposure (we are expanding into EMEA next year)

## Compensation

Base $185-220K + equity + standard benefits. Range reflects the seniority band, not the candidate.

## Process

- Recruiter screen (30 min)
- Hiring manager (45 min)
- Product case (60 min, take-home)
- Panel: engineering partner, design partner, growth marketing lead (45 min each)
- VP Product (45 min)
- References + offer
