# Preferred-qualification weighting

Each preferred qualification carries 1 point. Self-serve onboarding and technical fluency carry 2 points each — they are load-bearing for this role and the growth pod will feel the gap immediately if either is missing. Total possible: 9.

## Advance / hold / decline thresholds

- **ADVANCE:** required qualifications 100% met AND preferred score >= 4.
- **HOLD:** required qualifications met AND preferred score 2-3, OR any credential discrepancy (school, dates, title) that needs human verification.
- **DECLINE:** any required qualification missing, OR wrong seniority band for the role.

A HOLD means the résumé is interesting but cannot auto-advance to a phone screen. The hiring manager makes the call after reviewing the flagged item.
